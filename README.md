# Decarbonizing French Aviation: Addressing CO₂ Utilization & Modal Shift Challenges

## Why This Issue?
### Current State & Environmental Impact
- **France captures** 4.7 MtCO₂/year but allocates **<1% to SAF**, diverting **99% to enhanced oil recovery (EOR)**.
- **Missed Opportunity**: SAF production requires **3.88 kg CO₂ per kg of e-kerosene**. France’s unused CO₂ could produce **1.2 Mt/year of SAF** (30% of aviation fuel demand).

### Environmental Harm
- **EOR Extends Fossil Dependency**: CO₂ used for oil extraction offsets only **20% of emissions** from burned crude.
- **SAF Deficit**: Without CO₂ recycling, France imports **70% of SAF**, increasing lifecycle emissions by **25% (shipping).**

## Key Obstacles

| Category       | Challenges |
|---------------|------------|
| **Regulatory** | EU’s 2041 ban on industrial CO₂ for e-fuels jeopardizes cement/steel partnerships. |
| **Economic**   | EOR generates **€50/t revenue** vs. SAF’s **€20/t (no subsidies)**. |
| **Technological** | No domestic DAC facilities; reliance on U.S. startups like Climeworks. |

## Opportunities
- **Existing Infrastructure**: 45 industrial plants (cement, steel) can retrofit CO₂ capture for SAF.
- **Policy Alignment**: France 2030’s **€1.2B aerospace fund** prioritizes CO₂-to-fuel R&D.

## Diagnostic Diagram
```
[CO₂ Sources] → (4.7 Mt/year)  
    ├→ EOR (99%) → Fossil Lock-in  
    └→ SAF (1%) → Emission Reductions  
```

## Strategy in Action
### Solutions
1. **Policy Overhaul**: Mandate **30% of captured CO₂** for SAF by 2030 via **Carbon Valorization Act**.
2. **Nuclear-DAC Synergy**: Co-locate DAC units at EDF reactors (e.g., Flamanville) for 24/7 operation.
3. **CO₂ Market Creation**: Launch **SAF-linked carbon contracts (€200/t floor price)** on Euronext.

## Modal Shift: Why Planes Over Trains?

### Root Causes in France
- **Time Efficiency**:
  - **Paris-Nice**: **TGV (5h40m) vs. flight (1h25m)**.
  - **Habit**: **68% of business travelers prioritize speed over sustainability**.
- **Cost & Accessibility**:
  - Airfare subsidies **€200M/year** vs. rail’s **€80M**.
  - **45% of regional airports lack direct TGV links**.
- **Comfort Perception**:
  - **52% of travelers rate planes as "more comfortable"** for luggage and seating.

## Environmental Impact Comparison

| Route                | CO₂ Emissions (kg/passenger) |
|----------------------|----------------------------|
| **Paris-Marseille (Plane)** | **120 kg** |
| **Paris-Marseille (TGV)** | **1.6 kg** |

## Action Plan for Modal Shift

| Timeline      | Actions                                      | Success Metrics |
|--------------|--------------------------------|----------------|
| **2025–2027** | Ban flights **under 2h** (extended from current **1.5h ban**) <br> Add **15 TGV routes** | **20% shift to rail** |
| **2028–2035** | Tax **kerosene €0.30/liter** <br> Build **10 airport-TGV hubs** | **50% rail share for <500km trips** |
| **2036–2040** | Full **rail electrification** <br> AI-optimized **multimodal tickets** | **90% rail dominance for domestic travel** |

## Success Indicators

| Year | Metric                        | Target |
|------|------------------------------|---------|
| **2030** | SAF production from CO₂ | **1.5 Mt/year** |
| **2035** | CO₂ utilization rate for SAF | **50%** |
| **2040** | Aviation emissions vs. 2025 | **92% reduction** |

France’s dual focus on **CO₂ recycling** and **modal shift** creates a blueprint for systemic decarbonization. By aligning **industrial policy** with **behavioral nudges**, the nation can transform aviation from a **climate liability** to a **circular economy showcase**.
# Storyboard for Future Aviation (2040)

This storyboard visualizes the future of aviation in 2040, focusing on sustainability and the successful implementation of decarbonization strategies in France.

## Overview

The storyboard is designed in an anime style and highlights the achievements and visual outcomes of a sustainable aviation industry. It showcases the transformation of the aviation sector through the use of Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF), high-speed rail dominance, clean energy infrastructure, and significant emission reductions.

## Scenes and Panels

### Scene 1: Sustainable Aviation Hub
- **Panel 1**: A futuristic airport with sleek, modern architecture. Planes are powered by Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF).
- **Panel 2**: Close-up of a plane with a label indicating it uses 100% recycled $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ for fuel.
- **Panel 3**: Passengers boarding the plane, with a sense of pride and satisfaction in contributing to sustainability.

### Scene 2: High-Speed Rail Dominance
- **Panel 1**: A bustling train station with high-speed TGV trains.
- **Panel 2**: Passengers enjoying a comfortable and efficient journey on the train.
- **Panel 3**: A graph or infographic showing 90% rail dominance for short-haul travel.

### Scene 3: Clean Energy Infrastructure
- **Panel 1**: Direct Air Capture (DAC) facilities integrated with nuclear plants, operating smoothly.
- **Panel 2**: Scientists and engineers monitoring the DAC process, ensuring continuous $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ capture.
- **Panel 3**: A visual representation of $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ being converted into SAF.

### Scene 4: Reduced Emissions
- **Panel 1**: A before-and-after comparison graph showing a 92% reduction in aviation emissions from 2025 to 2040.
- **Panel 2**: A celebratory scene with policymakers, industry leaders, and citizens acknowledging the success.
- **Panel 3**: A serene sky with fewer contrails, symbolizing reduced environmental impact.

## Visual Elements

- **Color Scheme**: Vibrant greens and blues to emphasize sustainability and progress.
- **Symbols**: Recycling arrows, train icons, and clean energy symbols.
- **Text**: Captions and speech bubbles to explain the achievements and their impact.

## Animation

- **Movement**: Animate the planes taking off smoothly, trains moving swiftly, and DAC facilities in operation.
- **Transitions**: Use smooth transitions to connect the different aspects of the sustainable future.

## Purpose

This storyboard aims to inspire and inform viewers about the potential of a sustainable aviation industry. It serves as a visual representation of the goals and outcomes of decarbonization efforts, highlighting the importance of innovation, policy, and public engagement in achieving a greener future.
